SCREENING METRICS / PROPOSED V1

Useful, clearly defined outputs

  • Downward airflow: integrate downward velocity over a horizontal room cross-section at the recorded height. Report in CMM and CFM.
  • Reverse flow: integrate upward velocity separately. Signed net flow can cancel in a closed room.
  • Air speed & coverage: area-weighted speed and the fraction of the plane with downward speed ≥ 0.5 m/s.
  • Blade load: shaft-axis torque and aerodynamic shaft power. Electrical input needs motor efficiency data.

The numerical reduction functions are tested. OpenFOAM extraction and engineering qualification are still pending.
